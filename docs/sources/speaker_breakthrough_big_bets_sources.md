## Discovery Notes

| search_area | result | decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modern pretrained TSE | ClearerVoice, WeSep, USEF-TSE, and penta TSE are the most actionable public candidates found. | ClearerVoice/WeSep/USEF deserve feasibility checks; penta remains retired as a drop-in because local scoring was poor. |
| Real-world TSE benchmark | REAL-T directly targets real conversational mixtures and emphasizes domain mismatch. | Use its lesson as a guardrail: do not trust LibriMix-only wins without Discord/call-domain word speaker accuracy. |
| Joint diarization-ASR | Sortformer, Reverb, and JEDIS-style work show the direction, but deployability varies by model availability and license. | Treat as a separate larger project, not another post-hoc speaker-label tweak. |
| Widened Sortformer checkpoints | Ultra-Sortformer 5spk/8spk checkpoints are runnable through the existing NeMo scorer and directly test whether the official 4spk cap is limiting this corpus. | Keep as adaptation/backbone candidates; the 5spk off-the-shelf result improves overall accuracy but remains below clean-source oracle slices. |
| Speaker-tagged SA-ASR | The Qwen3-ASR 1.7B AMI finetune is runnable and emits speaker tags, while TagSpeech is architecturally stronger but packaging-blocked in the current snapshot. | Qwen is useful as a cheap SA-ASR smoke but not a champion; TagSpeech/Reverb require access or runnable code before local evaluation. |
| Enrollment-conditioned target ASR | SE-DiCoW is the strongest runnable objective match found: mono mixture plus enrollment directly produces that speaker's words. | Use it as the adaptation backbone, but separately measure blind target activity because oracle diarization can hide the deployment bottleneck. |
| Joint versus per-target decoding | SA-DiCoW validates joint speaker-attributed decoding, while its real-meeting results preserve a strong case for separate enrollment-conditioned target passes. | For this fixed, small roster, prefer per-target decoding plus joint activity/confidence training before attempting an unavailable 918M-parameter joint checkpoint. |
